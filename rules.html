The Dynamic ELO Framework

4-Digit Scaling & Volatility Engine
Core Mathematics

The platform implements a serverless, 4-digit logarithmic rating engine calibrated specifically to factor discovery speed and prime decomposition precision. Every user starts at an onboarding baseline of 500 ELO (Tier 11 · Composite Apprentice).

Official ELO Delta Formulation
ΔR = K × (Net Accuracy% − E) × V
Where E = 1 / [1 + 10^((R_baseline − R_user) / 400)] and V = Allocated_Time / Expended_Time
  • Onboarding Baseline: All new profiles initialize strictly at 500 ELO, granting open access across foundational tiers without immediate firewall blocks.
  • Dynamic Volatility (K): K scales with active daily streak volume: K = 32 × (1 + log10(Streak + 1)). Maintaining consecutive daily runs doubles rating growth potential.
  • High-Tier Hardening (1800+ ELO): At or above 1800 ELO, a diminishing-returns dampener 1800 / R_user scales positive point yields, while rating losses remain 100% unshielded.
  • Inactivity Decay: Competitors at ≥ 1800 ELO idle for ≥ 14 days incur -10 ELO weekly penalties until competitive activity is re-anchored.
💡 Rule Insight: Quick sub-second factor inputs amplify the Velocity index (V up to 3.5×), rewarding pure mental calculation agility.
