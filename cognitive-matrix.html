📐 Therapeutic Number Theory Remediation Heuristics

Master these deterministic mathematical shortcuts to bypass mental latency roadblocks, counter palindromic prime traps, and decompose high-magnitude composites under pressure.

⚡

Universal 1001 Block Rule (7, 11, 13)

N = 1000a + b ≡ b - a (mod 1001) • Since 1001 = 7 × 11 × 13

Partition large 4 to 6-digit integers into 3-digit groups from right to left. Subtract the thousands block from the units block. If the result is divisible by 7, 11, or 13, the original number shares that exact prime divisor.

🎯

Fermat Difference of Squares

N = a² - b² = (a - b)(a + b) • Check a = ⌈√N⌉, ⌈√N⌉ + 1...

When encountering an odd composite whose prime factors are close in value (e.g., 143 = 12² - 1² = 11 × 13, 323 = 18² - 1² = 17 × 19), test squares just above N. If a² - N is a perfect square b², factorization is instant.

🛡️

Even-Length Palindromic Elimination

∑ d_odd - ∑ d_even ≡ 0 (mod 11) • 11 is the ONLY even-digit palindromic prime

Every palindrome with an even number of digits (e.g. 1221, 5335, 7887) is strictly composite and always divisible by 11. Never hesitate or attempt trial division by 2, 3, or 5 on even-length palindromes.

🔄

Rapid Binary Factor Halving

N = 2ⁿ × M • Rapid Bitwise Halving until M is strictly odd

For composites with large powers of 2 (e.g., 288, 576, 432), aggressively strip all 2s before inspecting remaining odd components. 288 → 144 → 72 → 36 → 18 → 9 → 3² yields 2⁵ × 3² in under 1.2 seconds.

✨

Sub-Second Digital Root Triage

Digital Root: DR(N) ≡ N (mod 9) • Sum digits while casting out 9s

Instantly eliminate 9s, pairs summing to 9 (e.g. 4+5, 3+6, 2+7), and check remaining digit residue. If remaining sum is 3, 6, or 9, pull prime factor 3 immediately without second-attempt penalties.

📏

√N Ceiling Bound Triage

If N is composite, smallest factor p ≤ ⌊√N⌋

Never trial divide past the square root ceiling. For N = 391, √391 ≈ 19.77. You only need to verify primes {2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17}. Testing 17 immediately yields 391 = 17 × 23, avoiding endless trial cycles.
